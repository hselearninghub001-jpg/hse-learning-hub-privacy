Welcome to HSE Learning Hub (“we,” “our,” or “us”), an educational mobile application owned and managed by Muhammad Haroon. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our application.

HSE Learning Hub provides educational materials and practice resources related to Health, Safety and Environment (HSE), including:

  • Safety Officer Course

  • OSHA Course
  • HSE learning materials
  • Mock exams, practice questions and tests

The app does not require users to create an account or log in. The app owner does not intentionally request or collect personal details such as a user's name, email address, phone number or password through an account-registration system. The app owner also does not maintain a user-account database.
